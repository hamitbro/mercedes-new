Heritage

A History of Greatness

1886
Karl Benz patents the world's first gasoline-powered automobile — the Benz Patent-Motorwagen. A revolution is born that changes humanity forever.
1926
Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft merges with Benz & Cie to create Mercedes-Benz — a name that would become the global synonym for automotive excellence.
1954
The 300 SL "Gullwing" debuts — the world's fastest production car of its era and an enduring icon of design, innovation and engineering mastery.
1984
AMG officially partners with Mercedes-Benz, unleashing a new era of hand-built, high-performance vehicles engineered to the most extreme standards.
2024
The AMG GT 63 S E Performance arrives with F1-derived hybrid technology delivering 831 hp — the most powerful production AMG ever created, available now.
